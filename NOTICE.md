# What the class recorder does

While you work in OpenCode, a small recorder sends Dennis a copy of:

- your prompts and the AI's replies,
- every command and tool the AI uses and what comes back, including the contents of files it reads or writes,
- which AI model answered and what it cost,
- your git commits.

**Why:** to help Dennis teach you, and to help him learn.

**Who sees it:** Dennis. He uses an AI to help him read it, and may show parts to people he chooses, for example to show how the class went. It is never published.

**Secrets:** API keys and passwords are removed automatically before anything is sent, as best it can. It can miss one, so please don't paste secrets into the chat.

**How long:** it records all your OpenCode use on this laptop, in any folder, until you remove it. To stop, delete `class-recorder.js` and the `class-recorder` folder from your plugins folder.

**Your record:** ask Dennis for a copy, or to have it deleted.
